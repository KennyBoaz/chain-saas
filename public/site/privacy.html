Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-24 · Chain Innovation Ltd

In short. Businesses use Chain to answer their own customers on WhatsApp. We process the messages and records of those conversations only to run the service for that business. We do not sell them, use them for advertising, or read them for our own purposes.

1. Who we are and our role

Chain Innovation Ltd ("we", "us") operates the Chain platform. There are two kinds of personal data on it, and our role differs:

  • Account data of the businesses that sign up to Chain. We decide how this is used, so we are the controller.
  • Conversation data of each business's own customers (the people who message that business on WhatsApp). The business decides why and how it is used, so the business is the controller and we are its processor. We act on the business's instructions, which are set out in our Terms of Service.

2. What we process

About the business and its staff: name, email address, business name, role, a one-way hash of the password (we never store the password itself), plan and subscription status, billing records, and the date, version and IP address when the terms were accepted.

WhatsApp connection: the business's WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID and the access token Meta issues when the business connects. The token is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).

About the business's customers: WhatsApp phone number and profile name; the messages they send and receive, including photos, voice notes, videos, documents and shared locations; delivery and read status; reactions; and the orders, appointments and support tickets created from the conversation. We also keep a short running history of each conversation so the AI assistant remembers context.

Technical data: IP address and browser details for sign-in sessions and security logs, and an audit log of important account actions.

3. Why we process it

  • To receive and send WhatsApp messages for the business and show them in its inbox.
  • To let the AI assistant write replies, take orders, book appointments and hand over to staff.
  • To run the account: sign-in, subscriptions, support, security, preventing abuse and meeting legal duties.

The business's customer data is used only for the business's own purposes of serving those customers. Our staff do not read your customers' conversations, except when you ask us for support, where it is needed to keep the service secure, or where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use conversation content to train our own models.

4. Consent and the business's responsibilities

When a business creates an account it confirms that it has read this policy and the Terms, understands the purposes above, and instructs us to process its customers' data for it. The business is responsible for having a lawful basis, including any consent or opt-in it needs from its own customers to message them on WhatsApp, and for telling those customers that automated replies may be used.

5. AI processing

To write a reply, the text of the conversation, and any photo or PDF the assistant is asked to look at, is sent to Google's Gemini API, which returns the reply. How Google handles that data is governed by Google's terms for the Gemini API.

6. Who else receives data

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform): to deliver messages. Meta's own privacy policy applies to its processing.
  • Google (Gemini API): as described above.
  • Our hosting provider that runs our servers and database.
  • Payment providers once online payments are switched on. Card and mobile-money details are handled by them, not stored by us.
  • Email or SMS providers if the business turns on alerts to its team.

Some of these providers operate outside Uganda, so data may be processed in other countries.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Each business's data is kept separate from every other business on the platform, access tokens are encrypted, and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, and we ask businesses to use strong passwords and to remove staff who no longer need access.

8. Keeping and deleting data

We keep data while the account is active. A business administrator can permanently delete a conversation, with its messages and files, from the console at any time. On a verified request we delete an account and its data within 30 days, except billing records we must keep by law. See Data deletion.

9. Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and object to its processing. If you are a customer of a business that uses Chain, please contact that business first, because it controls your data. We will help it respond. For account data, write to us at the address below.

10. Cookies

We use only the cookie and browser storage the console needs to keep you signed in and secure. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit.

11. Children

The service is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18.

12. Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page and, for material changes, ask account holders to accept the new version. Questions: privacy@chaininnovation.co.ug.
