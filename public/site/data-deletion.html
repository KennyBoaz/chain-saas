Data deletion

Version 2026-11-24 · Chain Innovation Ltd

This page explains how to have data removed from Chain, as required by Meta's platform terms and by Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

If you are a business using Chain

Delete a conversation yourself. A business administrator can permanently delete any conversation from the console (chat menu → Delete conversation). This removes the messages, files and the assistant's memory of that customer, and optionally their orders, appointments and tickets.

Delete your whole account. Email privacy@chaininnovation.co.ug from the address registered on the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm your identity and, once verified, permanently erase:

  • your business profile, staff accounts and sign-in details;
  • your WhatsApp connection and access token;
  • all customer records, conversations, messages and media files;
  • all orders, appointments, tickets and the assistant's conversation memory.

We keep only the invoice and billing records the law requires us to hold. These contain no conversation content.

If you messaged a business that uses Chain

That business decides what is done with your messages, and we process them on its instructions. Please ask the business to delete your data. You can also email us at privacy@chaininnovation.co.ug with your WhatsApp number and the business's name, and we will pass your request to the business and help it carry it out.

Deleting a conversation removes your messages, files and phone number from our systems for that business. Messages already delivered to your own WhatsApp stay on your device and on Meta's systems, which you can manage in WhatsApp.

How long it takes

We process verified requests within 30 days and confirm by email when the deletion is complete.
