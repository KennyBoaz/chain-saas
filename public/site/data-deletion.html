Chain SaaS

Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta's Platform Terms and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles, Chain SaaS provides you with the ability to delete your data.

How to Delete Your Data

If you are a Tenant (Business Owner):

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the platform by emailing us at privacy@chaininnovation.co.ug from the email address associated with your account.

When you request deletion, we will permanently erase:

  • Your business profile and login credentials.
  • Your WhatsApp connection tokens.
  • All customer contact records.
  • All chat history, messages, and media files.
  • All orders, appointments, and support tickets.

Note: We are required to retain basic invoice and billing records for legal tax purposes for up to 7 years, but these do not contain chat data.

If you are an End Customer (Messaging a Business on WhatsApp):

If you have messaged a business using our Service and wish to have your data deleted, you must contact that business directly. Since we are a data processor, we act on the instructions of the business.

If the business requests your data to be deleted, we will permanently erase your conversation history, phone number, and any associated orders or appointments from our databases.

Processing Time

Once a deletion request is received and verified, we will process it within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about data deletion, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@chaininnovation.co.ug.
