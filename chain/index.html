Questions

Before you start

How does the assistant learn about my business?

You write it down once in Settings: what you sell, your hours, delivery and payment terms, and the questions customers ask. The assistant answers from that, and says it will check with your team when it does not know.

Can I control what it says?

Yes. You set the tone and the rules, and it is told never to invent prices, stock or policies you have not given it. You can read every conversation and step in at any moment.

What happens when a customer needs a person?

The chat is flagged and your team is alerted. You take over from the inbox, the assistant goes quiet, and when you hand the chat back it knows what you told the customer.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. You connect your own WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official sign-up, from your dashboard.

Is my customers' data kept private?

Each business's chats, orders and files are kept separate from every other business on the platform. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
