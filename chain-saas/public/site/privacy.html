Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Chain Innovation Ltd ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Chain SaaS platform. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

  • Account Data: When you register, we collect your name, business name, email address, and billing information.
  • WhatsApp Data: When you connect your WhatsApp Business Account via Meta's Embedded Signup, we receive access tokens and phone number IDs. We do not store your WhatsApp password.
  • Customer Conversations: We store the messages exchanged between your business and your customers to provide the Service. This includes text, media attachments, and metadata (timestamps, phone numbers).
  • Usage Data: We may collect information on how the Service is accessed and used (e.g., browser type, IP address, pages visited).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To process your subscription payments.
  • To route WhatsApp messages to our AI engine (Google Gemini) to generate automated responses.
  • To notify you about changes to our Service.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To monitor the usage of our Service and detect technical issues.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the third-party providers necessary to run the Service:

  • Meta (WhatsApp): To send and receive messages on your behalf. Meta's Privacy Policy applies to their processing.
  • Google (Gemini AI): To process the text of incoming messages and generate AI replies.
  • Payment Processors: To securely process your subscription payments (e.g., Flutterwave).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. You may request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Please see our Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. We encrypt your WhatsApp Access Tokens using AES-256-GCM at rest. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@chaininnovation.co.ug.
